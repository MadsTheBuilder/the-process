## Overview
Warm paper, graphite ink, uneven hand-drawn panel border. Every shot is drawn on stroke by stroke, then held while a slow camera pan/zoom plays.

## Colors
Ink for all lines, pencil grey for hatching and secondary lines. Washes are pale and sparing: blue for Dhruv's jacket, khaki for uniforms. Red is only the balloon. Gold is only Golden Hours / time pressure.

## Typography
Oswald uppercase for speaker labels and supers, Space Mono for the spoken line. Caption bar sits under the panel.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do keep line wobble small and consistent; seeded, never random per render.
- Do not add colours outside the palette. Do not show the thin man's face or readable FIR text.
- Do not add elements the shot sheet does not describe.
